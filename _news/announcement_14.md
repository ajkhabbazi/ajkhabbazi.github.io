I received the **[Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program)** from the American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE)! It is a competitive award from ASHRAE's Research Administration Committee supporting graduate research in HVAC&R, with 10-25 granted annually worldwide.
