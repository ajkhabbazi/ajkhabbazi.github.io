<span class="home-bio-lead">Hey there! I'm a PhD candidate at [Purdue Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME), with a concentration in [Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/oigp/program/cigp/), advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/).</span> I work on **modeling**, **control**, **optimization**, and **machine learning** for **building energy systems**. My PhD work is supported in part by an ASHRAE [Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program).

In 2025 I interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), working on machine learning projects for the drive unit team. Before Purdue I completed my MS at [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), where I worked with Professor [Sunny Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) on mixing hydrogen into natural gas distribution pipelines, in an [NSERC](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/)-funded collaboration with [FortisBC](https://www.fortisbc.com/).
