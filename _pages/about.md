Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), with a concentration in [Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/oigp/program/cigp/), advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). I work on modeling, control, optimization, and machine learning for building energy systems, focusing on **reinforcement learning** and **predictive control**. I also work with Purdue's Energy and Utilities team on demand flexibility in campus buildings, and I contribute to research on power control systems for homes and to Google's TensorFlow Smart Buildings Simulator ([SBSim](https://github.com/google/sbsim)). My PhD work is supported in part by a [Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program) from the American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE).

In 2025 I interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), working on machine learning projects for the drive unit team. Before Purdue I completed my M.S. at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), where I worked with Professor [Sunny Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) on hydrogen blending in natural gas distribution networks, funded by [FortisBC](https://www.fortisbc.com/). I earned my B.S. with Highest Distinction at the University of Tabriz in Iran. See my [research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) for the projects.
