Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), with a concentration in [Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/oigp/program/cigp/), advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). I work on modeling, control, optimization, and machine learning for building energy systems, focusing on **reinforcement learning** and **predictive control**. My research aims to advance the state of the art of practical automation systems for efficient, occupant- and grid-interactive buildings. I'm developing and demonstrating scalable algorithms in real buildings, minimizing data and engineering requirements. I also work with Purdue's Energy and Utilities team on demand flexibility in campus buildings, and I contribute to research on power control systems for homes. In 2025 I interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), working on machine learning projects for the drive unit team. My PhD work is supported in part by a [Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program) from the American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE).

Buildings use about a third of the world's energy, and much of that depends on how their systems are controlled. My contributions so far include the most extensive synthesis in the literature of experimental research in my field, as well as a result that identifies, explains, and resolves a widespread and long-standing error with measurement and verification during field experiments. I also contribute to Google's TensorFlow Smart Buildings Simulator ([SBSim](https://github.com/google/sbsim)).

Before Purdue I completed my M.S. at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), where I worked with Professor [Sunny Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) on hydrogen blending in natural gas distribution networks, funded by [FortisBC](https://www.fortisbc.com/). I earned my B.S. with Highest Distinction at the University of Tabriz in Iran.
