I am a PhD student in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), with a specialization in Computational Science and Engineering, and advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/).

My research focuses on reinforcement learning and predictive control for buildings, with an emphasis on model predictive control and data-enabled predictive control. My PhD research has been recognized with the [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program#:~:text=It%20is%20awarded%20once%20each,amount%20of%20up%20to%20%2410%2C000) Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award. More broadly, I am interested in applying machine learning to AI-enabled engineering problems. I previously interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), where I worked with the Drive Unit team on machine learning for quality assessment.

Prior to Purdue, I received my MS from the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), where I was advised by Professor [Sunny Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en), and earned my BS in Mechanical Engineering (Highest Distinction) in Iran.
