I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Purdue, also pursuing a minor in Computational Science and Engineering. I work at [Herrick Laboratories](https://engineering.purdue.edu/Herrick) in the [Kircher Research Group](https://kevinjkircher.com/). Previously, I earned my MS at the University of British Columbia with Prof. [Sunny Ri Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) and a BS in Mechanical Engineering with Highest Distinction in Iran. I also interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), contributing to machine learning and quality assessment projects for the Drive Unit team.

My research centers on machine learning, optimization, and advanced control strategies for intelligent buildings and [distributed energy resources](https://kevinjkircher.com/distributed-energy-resources/). I work on both modeling and field demonstrations to improve energy efficiency, cut costs, and enhance occupant comfort. I’ve authored a [review paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.126459) surveying 100+ papers on field demonstrations of model predictive control and reinforcement learning for HVAC, and was awarded the ASHRAE [Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program#:~:text=It%20is%20awarded%20once%20each,amount%20of%20up%20to%20%2410%2C000.).
