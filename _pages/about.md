Hi there! I am a PhD candidate in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), with a [concentration in Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/oigp/program/cigp/), advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). I work on modeling, control, optimization, and machine learning for building energy systems. I am currently looking for **research internships**, and I am open to **visiting researcher** positions as well.

Buildings use about a third of the world's energy, and much of that depends on how their systems are controlled. My research advances practical automation systems for efficient, occupant- and grid-interactive buildings, focusing on reinforcement learning and data-driven predictive control. So far my PhD work has produced the most extensive synthesis of the experimental literature in my field, and a forthcoming paper that identifies, explains, and resolves a widespread, long-standing error in measurement and verification during field experiments. Most of my current research focuses on developing and demonstrating scalable data-driven algorithms for high-performance building control. I also work on power control systems in homes and on demand flexibility in commercial buildings with Purdue's Energy and Utilities team. My PhD work is supported in part by a Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award from [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program).
