I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Purdue, also pursuing a minor in Computational Science and Engineering. I work at [Herrick Laboratories](https://engineering.purdue.edu/Herrick) with Prof. [Kevin J. Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). Previously, I earned my MS at the University of British Columbia with Prof. [Sunny R. Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) and a BS in Mechanical Engineering with Highest Distinction in Iran. I also interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), contributing to machine learning and quality assessment projects for the Drive Unit team.

My research centers on data-driven and model-based control strategies such as reinforcement learning (RL) and model predictive control (MPC), along with machine learning methods, to make buildings, energy systems, and other AI-driven applications smarter and more efficient. My PhD work has been recognized with the [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program#:~:text=It%20is%20awarded%20once%20each,amount%20of%20up%20to%20%2410%2C000) Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award.
