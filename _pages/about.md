I’m a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University, pursuing a minor in Computational Science and Engineering, and conducting research at [Herrick Laboratories](https://engineering.purdue.edu/Herrick) with Prof. [Kevin J. Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). My background includes an MS from the University of British Columbia with Prof. [Sunny R. Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) and a BS in Mechanical Engineering (Highest Distinction). I’ve also spent time as an intern at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), where I worked with the Drive Unit team on machine learning and quality assessment initiatives.

I work on reinforcement learning (RL) and model predictive control (MPC) for smart buildings and energy systems. I also explore broader machine learning (ML) methods in other AI-enabled applications. My PhD research has been recognized with the [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program#:~:text=It%20is%20awarded%20once%20each,amount%20of%20up%20to%20%2410%2C000) Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award.
