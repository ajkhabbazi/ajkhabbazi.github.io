I’m a PhD student in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), pursuing a specialization in Computational Science and Engineering, and advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). My background includes an MS from the University of British Columbia, where I was advised by Professor [Sunny Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mgUDmT8AAAAJ&hl=en) and a BS in Mechanical Engineering (Highest Distinction). I also interned at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/), where I worked with the Drive Unit team on machine learning and quality assessment initiatives.

I work on reinforcement learning (RL) and predictive control for buildings, including model predictive control (MPC) and data-driven predictive control (DDPC). I am also interested in applying broader machine learning (ML) methods to AI-enabled problems across domains. My PhD research has been recognized with the [ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program#:~:text=It%20is%20awarded%20once%20each,amount%20of%20up%20to%20%2410%2C000) Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award.
