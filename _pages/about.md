Glad you found your way here! I am a PhD candidate in [Mechanical Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), specializing in [Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/oigp/program/cigp/) and advised by Professor [Kevin Kircher](https://kevinjkircher.com/). I am currently looking for **research intern** and **visiting researcher** positions in industry or academia.

Buildings use about a third of the world's energy, and much of that depends on how their systems are controlled. Advanced controllers, which use models, forecasts, and data to plan ahead rather than react, can cut it substantially, but they are not widely adopted in industry. My work targets that gap. I use **predictive control** and **reinforcement learning** for building energy systems, drawing on machine learning, control theory, modeling and simulation, and field demonstrations. I apply these to HVAC control, measurement and verification, demand flexibility, and home electrification, aiming for results that owners and operators can trust and methods that run across many buildings without an expert tuning each one. This work is supported in part by a Graduate Student Grant-in-Aid Award from **[ASHRAE](https://www.ashrae.org/communities/student-zone/scholarships-and-grants/graduate-student-grant-in-aid-award-program)**.
